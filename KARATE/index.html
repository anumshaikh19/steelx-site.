WEEK STARTS.

WHAT AGE CAN CHILDREN START?+

From around two and a half years. The youngest students train in small groups with focus drills, games and etiquette.

I HAVE NEVER TRAINED BEFORE.+

Most beginners start with the basics. You do not need previous experience.

DO I NEED A UNIFORM?+

Comfortable clothes and water are enough for your first session.

CAN ADULTS TRAIN?+

Yes. Karate, calisthenics and self defence options are available for adults.
