Kumite 04 · Intermediate Kumite

Jiyu-Ippon-Kumite

Semi-free one-step

Freestyle one-step sparring, also known as semi-free sparring, is very similar to basic one-step sparring, except that in jiyu-ippon, both participants start in jiyu-kamae (freestyle position).

After each attack and counter-attack, the participants return to jiyu-kamae. Sometimes more than one counter-attack is executed. Jiyu-ippon is usually taught to intermediate and advanced karateka.

Although moving closer to actual free-sparring, this remains a form of yakusoku-kumite (announced sparring). It bridges the gap between basic announced sparring and jiyu-kumite.
