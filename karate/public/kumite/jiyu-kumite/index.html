Jiyu-Kumite

Freestyle sparring

Free-sparring is not part of the yakusoku set of kumite. Simply put, free sparring is practice fighting. Participants begin in freestyle position and fight each other using full speed attacks and defenses.

Only the lightest contact is permitted, therefore a high level of skill is necessary. Free-sparring is generally reserved for advanced belts only. It provides an excellent opportunity to test one's skills against another individual with complete freedom of movement.

Through jiyu-kumite practice, one prepares for shiai-kumite.
