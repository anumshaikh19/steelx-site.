Kumite 03 · Intermediate Kumite

Kihon-Ippon-Kumite

1-step sparring

Basic one-step sparring is normally taught at the beginner and intermediate levels. The attacker may use only one attack with one step. Many different attack forms can be used, such as various strikes and kicks, but only one at a time. After each single attack, both opponents must restart.

One-step sparring is very useful in teaching the student how to counter-attack quickly, preventing the opponent from launching a second attack. The defender learns tai sabaki, or body shifting, shifting off the axis of attack and delivering a counter-attack.

As in all kihon-kumite sets, the defender starts in a natural stance and basic technique is maintained throughout. Variations include gaeshi-ippon-kumite (returning one-step sparring) and okuri-ippon-kumite (sliding sparring).
