Kihon-Sanbon-Kumite

3-step sparring

Basic three-step sparring is almost exactly the same as five-step sparring except that the number of steps has been reduced to three.

Three-step sparring takes up less time and space than five-step and is generally considered more efficient. It still teaches basic punching and blocking technique, and it trains the student to maintain proper distance while moving.
