Shiai-Kumite

Competition sparring

Competition sparring is the kumite performed at tournaments. It is like a sport, having rules and regulations, and of course, a winner and a loser.

In JKA Shotokan karate, shiai-kumite is either shobu-ippon-kumite or sanbon-shobu-kumite. Shobu-ippon is a one-point match. The winner is the person who scores an ippon with a perfect technique. A less-than-perfect technique may score a waza-ari, or half-point; two waza-ari equal ippon.

Ippon techniques are usually awarded when the opponent is knocked off balance before an effective attack or counter-attack, when consecutive attacks reach the target, when the opponent makes no attempt at defense, or when an attack is evaded while an effective counter-attack is delivered.

Sanbon-shobu-kumite is a three-point match and means best 2 out of 3, or the majority of points. It is usually reserved for the final match of JKA-style competitions.
